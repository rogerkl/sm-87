# A program change on the drums' channel chooses their pattern, as the HR-16's MIDI PROGRAM CHANGE: off until the
# drum page's PROGRAM CHANGE turns it on. Stopped: at once. Two parts of 8 beats (the drums' default), each with a
# program change recorded on its downbeat: played, part 00's chooses pattern 03 from the top, and part 01's, queued
# after it, 07 on its first beat (the drums starting over with it), not a pattern later. One added with EDIT, as the
# manual says, does the same. Kept with SAVE.
# 120 BPM: a beat is 500 ms.
wait 600
# off: not taken
midi C9 05
wait 30
expect drprog 0
expect drpat 0
# the drum page's PROGRAM CHANGE (ALGO 8): K1 on
tap ARP
wait 30
turn ALGO 8
wait 30
expect drfn 8
turn K1 1
wait 30
expect drprog 1
shot drumprog.png
# stopped: pattern 05 at once
midi C9 05
wait 30
expect drpat 5
tap ARP
wait 30
expect drfn 0

wait 30

tap REC
wait 30
expect armed 1
tap PLAY
wait 1840
midi C9 03
wait 30
expect drpat 3
wait 4050
tap PLAY

tap PLAY
