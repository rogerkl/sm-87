# A pattern's own sounds (hr16/hr.h; the HR-16's VOICE, TUNE and MIX stored with a pattern), on the drum page's PAD
# SOUND: a pattern without its own plays the global set-up (an edit is the set-up's); REC keeps the pads' sounds with
# the pattern (an edit then the pads' until stored: another pattern taken, gone); the pads take a pattern's sounds as
# it is chosen, and as it comes in playing; SEL + REC lets its own go; COPY to an empty pattern takes them too; kept
# across a power cycle. The kit: KICK SNARE CLHAT OPHAT CYMBL (sounds 0..4); pad 1 KICK.
wait 600
tap ARP
wait 30
turn ALGO 6
wait 30
expect drfn 6
expect drsndp 0
expect drown 0
# a global edit: the kick pad to the snare's sound, the global set-up's too
turn K1 1
wait 30
expect padsnd1 1
expect stdsnd1 1
shot drumsnd_global.png
# REC: kept with pattern 00
tap REC
wait 30
expect patown0 1
expect drown 1
expect dredited 0
# its own edited: the pads', not the global set-up's, until stored
turn K1 1
wait 30
expect padsnd1 2
expect stdsnd1 1
expect dredited 1
wait 1500
shot drumsnd_edited.png
tap REC
wait 30
expect dredited 0
turn K2 3 20
wait 30
expect padtune1 3
expect dredited 1

turn PRESET 1
wait 30

expect drown 0
expect padsnd1 1
expect padtune1 0
wait 1500
shot drumsnd_other.png
turn PRESET -1
wait 30

expect drown 1
expect padsnd1 2
expect padtune1 0
expect dredited 0
