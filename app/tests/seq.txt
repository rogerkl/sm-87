# The MMT-8 workflow: arm, a bar's count-in, a take from MIDI in that sets the part's length, play it back, mute it,
# choose the record track, a song of two steps, and all of it kept across a power cycle. 120 BPM: a beat is 500 ms.
wait 600
tap SEQ
wait 30
expect part 0
expect tracks 0
expect beats 0
shot seq_empty.png
# REC arms track 1 (the lowest); PLAY starts the count-in
tap REC
wait 30
expect armed 1
expect rectrack 1
tap PLAY
wait 30
expect seq 1
shot seq_countin.png
wait 2000
expect seq 2
expect take 1

wait 250
midi TRS 90 40 50

wait 220
shot seq_rec.png

tap PLAY
