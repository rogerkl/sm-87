# The HR-16's STEP EDIT (ALGO on the drum page): SELECT from place to place (the 1/16 grid, every hit, one hit at a
# time where there are more, round at the ends), a pad played (MIDI in on channel 10, or a white key) going in at
# the place, K2 its velocity, K1 its place (off the grid: SELECT stops there), K3 its pad, REC erasing it, K4 the
# grid; PLAY leaves it. Pattern 00, new: 8 beats, 768 clocks.
wait 600
tap ARP
wait 30
turn ALGO 1
wait 30
expect drfn 1
expect drstep 0
expect drstepn 0
# the kick on the one
midi 99 24 64
wait 30
expect drhits 1
expect drsteppad 1
expect drstepvel 100
expect drbeats 8
# on a 1/16: the snare and the closed hat there, the last one played shown
turn SELECT 1
wait 30
expect drstep 24
midi 99 26 32
midi 99 2A 28
wait 30
expect drstepn 2
expect drsteppad 3
expect drstepi 2
shot step.png
# back: the snare (a hit at a time), then the kick; back from the one: the pattern's last step, 744
turn SELECT -1
wait 30
expect drstep 24
expect drsteppad 2
turn SELECT -1
wait 30
expect drstep 0
expect drsteppad 1
turn SELECT -1
wait 30
expect drstep 744
expect drstepn 0
turn SELECT 1
wait 30
expect drstep 0
# K2 the velocity; K1 its place, 5 clocks on (off the grid: SELECT stops there too)
turn K2 -20 20
wait 30

turn K1 5 20
wait 30

expect drsteppad 1
turn SELECT -1

turn K3 1

tap REC

expect drstepn 0
# K4 the grid: 1/8 (0, 48, ...): from 0 on, the hits at 24 (one, then the other), then 48

# SEL + SELECT: by beats
press SEL
