# The record track as the MMT-8's: REC arms the part's lowest empty track; REC + the armed track's key again leaves
# none chosen (REC then says so) until REC + a key chooses one; another part, the lowest empty again. REC in a first
# take sets its length, to the nearest beat, and the part plays on. 120 BPM: a beat is 500 ms.
wait 600
tap SEQ
wait 30
tap REC
wait 30
expect armed 1
expect rectrack 1
# REC + track 1, the armed one: none
press REC
tapkey w0
release REC
wait 30
expect armed 0
tap REC
wait 30
expect armed 0
# REC + track 3: it, armed
press REC
tapkey w2
release REC
wait 30
expect armed 1
expect rectrack 3
