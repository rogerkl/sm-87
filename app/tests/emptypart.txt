# An empty part has no end: played, its beats count on, and a part chosen meanwhile starts on the next beat. Stopping
# clears a part queued. A first take on an empty part runs until stopped (that sets its length): PRESET waits.
# 120 BPM: a beat is 500 ms.
wait 600
tap SEQ
wait 30
expect part 0
expect beats 0
# PLAY on the empty part 00 (not armed): on past where a part would end
tap PLAY
wait 2700
expect seq 2
expect part 0
# PRESET to part 01 (empty too): queued, and on the next beat it plays
turn PRESET 1
expect nextpart 1
wait 520
expect part 1
expect nextpart -1
# again from the top: part 02 queued in the first beat, then stopped before the second: the queue goes
tap PLAY
tap PLAY
wait 100
turn PRESET 1
expect nextpart 2
tap PLAY
wait 30
expect seq 0
expect nextpart -1
expect part 1
# a first take on the empty part 01: REC, PLAY, the count-in (4 beats), then PRESET: not queued, said
tap REC
expect armed 1
tap PLAY
wait 2500
expect take 1
turn PRESET 1
expect nextpart -1
expect part 1
wait 400
expect part 1
tap PLAY
wait 30
expect seq 0
