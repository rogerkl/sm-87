# The kit the samples built in bring (test/run.sh: tools/mkrom.py --emu ... --kit, two sounds of the 440 Hz sine):
# it replaces the firmware's own, its sounds and its pads' set-up. Pad 1 KICK (BEEP, note 36), pad 2 HIGH (BEEP
# tuned up 7, level 50, pan -2, note 40, the hats' group, two voices); the pads after them none, each on the first
# General MIDI note left. A pad's sound changed and saved is kept: the same kit.
wait 600
expect kit 2
expect padsnd1 0
expect padnote1 36
expect padsnd2 0
expect padnote2 40
expect padtune2 7
expect padlevel2 50
expect padpan2 -2
expect padsnd3 -1
expect padnote3 38
# pad 2 from its note (40, 28 in hex) on the drum channel: a voice, then the sound
midi 99 28 64
wait 30
expect drvoices 1
wav bankkit_pad2.wav
wait 300
wavstop
wait 500
expect drvoices 0
# PAD SOUND: pad 1 to the kit's second sound (BOP), the global set-up's; saved, and kept across a power cycle
tap ARP
wait 30
turn ALGO 6
wait 30
expect drfn 6
turn K1 1
wait 30
expect padsnd1 1
expect stdsnd1 1
shot bankkit_sound.png
tap SAVE
wait 300
reboot
wait 600
expect kit 2
expect stdsnd1 1
expect padsnd1 1
expect padnote2 40
