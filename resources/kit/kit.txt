# SM-87's drum kit: the HR-16's sounds and its pads' set-up. Every build makes it a header of the firmware's
# (app/fm1.toml gen: tools/mkkit.py); run mkkit.py by hand to see what it holds and takes.
#
#   sound NAME FILE [vel=N]
#       a WAV file in this folder, mixed down to mono, at one of the sampler's rates (or resampled to the nearest),
#       12 bits as the sampler keeps it. NAME: up to 6 characters. vel: velocity's say in its level, 0..99 (the
#       sampler's VEL>LV; 60 unless told)
#   pad NAME SOUND [tune=N] [level=N] [pan=N] [note=N] [group=N] [poly=N]
#       the pads in order, up to 16 (the white keys from the lowest); SOUND a sound's NAME. tune: semitones
#       (-24..24), level 0..99 (85), pan -3..3 (0), note: its MIDI note (General MIDI's), group: pads that cut each
#       other (the hats: 1), poly: its voices at once, 1 or 2 (the HR-16's crash: 2)
#
# A pad saved keeps its sound by number: put new sounds at the end of the list.

sound KICK  BD.wav
sound SNARE Snare.wav
sound CLHAT CH.wav
sound OPHAT OH.wav
sound CYMBL Cymbal.wav

pad KICK  KICK  note=36
pad SNARE SNARE note=38
pad CLHAT CLHAT note=42 group=1
pad OPHAT OPHAT note=46 group=1
pad CRASH CYMBL note=49 poly=2
pad RIDE  CYMBL note=51 tune=-3 poly=2
